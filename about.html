I'm Wesley Ratko, a newsroom developer and data journalist at the San Antonio Express‑News. I blend urban planning, data analysis, and visual storytelling to explain how the natural and built environments shape life in San Antonio. My focus: turning complex datasets into clear, useful reporting and interactive visuals.

I started as an urban planner in Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, where data and mapping drove growth planning. After my wife joined Ohio University's faculty, I returned to graduate school for information graphics and interactive design— training that sharpened how I pair data, code, and design to serve readers.
